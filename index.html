⚡ Zapier Integration

Connect

Connect Fed-Poster to thousands of apps (Substack, Shopify, YouTube, RSS, Google Sheets, and more) via Zapier webhooks.

⚡ How it works: Create a Zap in Zapier. Use Webhooks by Zapier as the Trigger or Action. Enter the URL below. Fed-Poster receives the data and posts to your selected networks.

YOUR_ZAPIER_WEBHOOK_URL
🔗 Send POST requests here. Requires an API key in the X-API-Key header.
••••••••••••••••
🔑 Your key is stored locally. Keep it secret!
  1. Create a new Zap in Zapier.
  2. Choose a Trigger (e.g., "New Subscriber" in Substack, "New Video" in YouTube, or "New Row" in Google Sheets).
  3. Choose "Webhooks by Zapier" as the Action.
  4. Select "POST" as the method.
  5. Paste the Webhook URL from above.
  6. Add a Header: Key = X-API-Key, Value = your API key.
  7. In the "Data" field, send a JSON payload like:
    {"text": "Hello from Zapier!", "platforms": ["Telegram", "Bluesky"]}
  8. Test and turn on your Zap!

ℹ️ About Fed-Poster

Info

Fed-Poster is a multi‑platform publishing dashboard built for creators, developers, and power users.


📤 What it does:

  • Post to 21+ social networks with one click.
  • Supports direct APIs (Telegram, Bluesky, Mastodon, Discord, GitHub, Tumblr, DeviantArt, Patreon, Stoat) and proxies (Buffer, WordPress Jetpack).
  • Unified “Post All” button – sends your message to all selected platforms simultaneously.
  • Smart testing – verify credentials one‑by‑one, auto‑uncheck failing platforms.
  • Zapier integration – connect to thousands of apps via webhooks.
  • Completely self‑contained – runs in your browser, stores credentials locally.

🚀 Why Fed-Poster?

  • No subscription fees – you keep your tokens.
  • Lightweight, fast, and open‑source ready.
  • Designed for creators who want full control over their social presence.

Built with ❤️ by the Fed-Poster community.
